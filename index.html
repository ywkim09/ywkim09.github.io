01 / About

Machine learning for complex systems.

I have been a Postdoctoral Researcher at Uppsala University since 2026. My work focuses on machine learning methods that are adaptive, label-efficient, and useful in real-world settings. Since September 2026, I have been working on battery diagnosis using acoustic emission. I earned my Ph.D. in Network System Engineering from KTH Royal Institute of Technology and have collaborated with New York University and industry partners including Ericsson.
