01 / About

Research with real-world impact.

I am a Ph.D. researcher in Network System Engineering at KTH Royal Institute of Technology, working at the intersection of machine learning and network security. My research explores how to detect threats with limited labels, adapt to changing environments, and help analysts make timely decisions. I have also collaborated with New York University and industry partners including Ericsson.

02 / Selected work

Curious about the hard problems.

A selection of themes from my research and collaborations.

RESEARCH 01

Human-in-the-loop intrusion detection

Combining active learning and analyst feedback to improve cyber intrusion detection while making the best use of limited labeling effort.

Active learning · Security
RESEARCH 02

Adaptive detection under concept drift

Developing label-efficient network intrusion detection that can respond as data and attacker behavior change over time.

Anomaly detection · Adaptation
RESEARCH 03

Dynamic alert prioritization

Using a hidden Markov model framework with active learning to help security teams prioritize alerts for real-time situational awareness.

Decision-making · Security operations
INDUSTRY 04

Exploring GAN-based clustering approaches for federated learning in communication networks that continuously change.

Federated learning · GANs
